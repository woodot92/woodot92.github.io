About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego, advised by Barna Saha. I am broadly interested in the design and analysis of algorithms, fine-grained complexity, and combinatorics. My recent work focuses on counting and min-plus problems.

Previously, I received an M.S. in Pure Mathematics and bachelor's degrees in Honors Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Michigan. My undergraduate advisor was Thatchaphol Saranurak.
